# GV ANIMATION BIBLE — Track B (Fractal Necropolis)
Frame-accurate animation specs for stages 11-13. Time unit = sim ticks (60/s). `T` = `gv.t` (+`RA` when drawing for sub-tick smoothness), `ez`/`tl`/`spring`/`Pool`/`fbm`/`hash2`/`rootSegs`/`leader` come from `tools/gv_lib.js`. Every animation is **event-driven** (`gvEmit` names in GV_ENGINE_SPEC §2.4), **deterministic** (no `Math.random` in draw; flicker = `fbm(T*f+id*7,seed)`), **cheap** (cached sprites, pools, ≤ 3 high-alpha effects at once) and has an audio cue on the same frame. Visual language stays the game's neon-cartoon look: soft glows, rounded shapes, saturated rim light.

## Principles
P1 **Colour = meaning.** Danger/live = white-blue core `#eaffff` + cyan halo `#7ef0ff`. Safe/ambient = `th.a` at α ≤ .35. Spectral = bone-blue `#bcd8ff`. Earth `#8a6a4a`. Copper `#d98a4a`. Never use the danger colour on decor above α .25.
P2 **Anticipation ≥ action.** Every hazard's tell is longer and more visible than its active moment (tell ≥ 30 f; poses before impact).
P3 **Three layers per effect:** base (cached) → motion (spring / oscillator) → sparkle (additive pool particles). Max 3 effects with α > .5 on screen.
P4 **Easing vocabulary:** build-up `inQuad`; impact `outBack` / `outElastic`; settle `spring(k .12, d .82)`; fade `outQuart`; ambient `inOutSine`. Linear only for scrolling.
P5 **Frame grid:** durations are multiples of 2 f; cascades stagger 2-3 f per element.
P6 **Soft things squash, hard things recoil:** liquid/flame/soil deform; stone micro-shakes (≤ 3 px) and rebounds.
P7 **Audio = animation:** `snd(freq,dur,type,vol,slide)` / `noise(dur,vol,freq)` on the event frame; positional via the `px` argument for sources on screen.
P8 **Readable in the dark:** anything that can hurt emits light (registered in `GVH.lights`) and survives `gvRim()`.

## Catalogue (ID → trigger → timeline → layers → particles → sound → camera/light)
**A01 Capacitor jar + liquid** (always; part 10). Jar = glass rounded rect, width .45·slab (28-52 px), 24 px tall, 4 cells; level `L=slabJar(q)` quantised to 16 steps for the sprite cache. Surface wave `y=top+(1-L)*h+amp*sin(x*.35+T*.12)`, `amp=1.5+2.5·slosh` where `slosh` is a `spring` kicked by landings (+.6) and events. Colour ramp: `L<.5` teal `#2bd9c7` → `<.9` cyan `#7ef0ff` → white-cyan `#eaffff`. Bubbles: spawn rate ∝ L (≤ 3/s), 1 px circles clipped to the jar. Cell lines at 25/50/75 %: flash 6 f (`ez.pulse`) when `L` crosses. Copper coil on the slab ends glows `α=.6·L²`. Hum: `snd(180+260L,.05,'sine',.012)` every 40 f only within 400 px of the player.
**A02 Slab states** (events `slab:warn|live|disch`).
 · WARN 48 f: f0-6 recoil (slab top dips 2 px, `outBack` back) + jar white flash; f6-48 two or three arcs crawl along the top edge (`leader` depth 3, jitter 6 px, reseed every 4 f: seed `id*97+(T>>2)`), α `ez.inQuad(u)·.9`; micro-shake ±1 px @30 Hz from f24; halo "crackle band" 14 px above the slab with hatch flicker; whine `snd(600→1500 over 48 f,'triangle',.02)`. No damage.
 · LIVE 150 f: top face white-blue gradient pulsing with `fbm`; 4 arcs reseeded every 2 f; sparks 1.5/f (life 18-30 f, white→cyan, gravity .15); 5 ion streams rising 20-36 px; jar white flicker; light r150 ±6 % breathing; loop `snd(120,.1,'square',.018)` every 6 f + hashed noise crackles. Last 20 f: arcs thin out (readable end).
 · DISCHARGE 24 f: f0 `gvJuice({fl:10,shake:6})`; 2 rings (slow `m 90`, fast `m 50`) `#bfe9ff`; jar implosion (level 100 %→0 in 10 f `outQuart` + vacuum flash); slab "kick" +3 px then `spring`; 8 radial bolts 12→60 px fading in 8 f; 24 sparks; `noise(.25,.08,5000)+snd(900,.2,'sawtooth',.05,-700)`.
 · REST: dim glow decays 30 f; coil colour white→copper via `tl`.
**A03 Player zap** (event `zap`; `hurt()` already gives shake 12, hit-stop 4, ring, burst, knockback). Add: 36 f of 4-6 short arcs around the body (`leader` depth 2, 14-22 px, reseed every 2 f); 12 f halo r 40 white-cyan α .5→0; `pop(x,y-10,'ZAP!','#bfe9ff')`; world "dims for a heartbeat": darkness α ×1.15 for 14 f; `snd(1800,.1,'sawtooth',.07,-1500)+noise(.12,.09,6000)+snd(70,.2,'sine',.1)`.
**A04 Charged enemy** (`charge:on|off`). Halo r `1.1·e.w` α .35 with `fbm` flicker; 2 orbiting sparks (angle `T*.25+id`, r `.7·e.w`) with 4-frame trails; 2 short arcs per 3 f; speed afterimages every 3 f into `Pool(48)` (additive rounded-rect silhouettes, α .35→0 in 9 f); on-charge: 12 f white flash, `ring m 40`, rising chirp `snd(600→1400,.12,'triangle')`; last 60 f: flicker ×3 and shrink; end: 6-spark puff + tick. First time: `gvHint('chg',…)`.
**A05 Overload chain** (`chain`). 8 f bolt (`lgBolt` main width 3, fork at midpoint): f0-2 full, f2-8 width 3→1 and α 1→0; target flash + 8 sparks + small ring; `snd(1500,.08,'square',.05,-1000)`.
**A06 Pools** (`pool:tell|live|idle`). Idle: 12 px glossy strip, 3 moving specular streaks (40-90 px, α .12-.25, `fbm`-driven), hashed ripple ellipses, stretched moon reflection (5 stacked α), vertical reflection streaks under every light above it. TELL 40 f: 6 bright dots race from the centre outward (`inQuad`), thin cyan line ramps, surface shiver 1→2.5 px, hum rising, hairline arcs from f28. LIVE 36 f: zig-zag lattice arcs along the surface (reseed 2 f), surface lifted 3 px with ion streams up to 22 px ("don't stand here"), light r = half width. Exit: arcs shrink to centre in 6 f + outward ripple.
**A07 Cables.** Fractal polylines baked once; **growth reveal** at stage start: revealed length `ez.outQuart` over 90 f, staggered per cable. Charging: a faint pulse dot every 90 f (1.2 px/f). Discharge: fast pulse (6 px/f) with a 6-sample comet tail slab→pool. Storm pulses: 12 px/f bright, 10-sample tail; arrival → node flare (ring + 4 sparks on the receiving jar). Twigs brighten in a travelling wave `α+=exp(-((d-pos)/18)²)`.
**A08 Slab landing squash** (visual only; collision unchanged). On `pl.g` becoming a slab: draw offset spring `dy` (impulse 3 px, `k .14,d .8`), jar slosh +.8, 3 dust motes; takeoff rebound −1 px.
**A09 Cantor bridge** (`cantor:tell|anim`). Cells are hinged slabs: removal = fold around the cell's centre-x like a closing book: `scaleX=|cos θ|`, θ 0→π/2 in 10 f `inOutCubic`, lift 4 px·`pulse`; return = reverse with `outBack` (8 % overshoot, visual only — collision width stays ≤ w0). Tell (36 f): vanishing cells pulse violet α .2→.8 (`inQuad`) with hairline cracks (`rootSegs` depth 2 growing); returning cells show a dashed ghost outline marching (`dashOffset=T*.5`) and a shimmer fill; chime `snd(523·2^(i/9),.12,'triangle',.03)` staggered 3 f per cell. Rune wave: a 60 px light runs left→right along the cell tops (`(T*.9)%(W+60)`). **Keystones:** anchor cells 0,2,6,8 carry a pulsing gem (0.5 Hz) = "these never fall". Cascade: fold delay `|i-4|·2` f inside the 18 f anim. Under the bridge a faint Sierpiński triangle lights up one more recursion level per state (0 none → 1 → 2).
**A10 Sinking grave** (`grave:tremble|swallow|emerge`). `s=sk/max`. 0-.25 slight settle; .25-.5 tremble ±(2s) px @40 Hz (deterministic) + dirt trickle (rate 3s/f); .5-.75 cracks glow mint→amber (α s) + rumble `snd(55,.3,'sawtooth',.04)`; ≥ .6 two skeletal hands poke from the soil (finger pose `tl` curl→spread + `fbm` twitch). Swallow: remaining depth in 8 f (`inQuad`), hands clasp, brown ring `m 60`, 20 earth particles, `shake 4`, `noise(.3,.1,900)+snd(80,.3,'sawtooth',.08,-40)`. Gone: loose-soil disk with ripples + rune progress ring sweeping over `swallowT`, blinking in the last 60 f. Emerge: soil dome bulges (`spring`, 12 f) → slab rises `outBack`, 16-particle dirt fountain, thud, A08 settle.
**A11 Soul lantern** (`lamp:lit|flicker|dark`). Cage = damped pendulum: `ω+=-θ*.02; ω*=.985; θ+=ω`, idle sway from `fbm` (±.03 rad), star hit `ω+=±.12`. Flame = 3 nested teardrops, height `14+6·fbm(T*.2)`, lean `θ·20`, core white/`th.a`; lit halo r 34 pulsing; dark = dim ember. 3 wisps/s spiral up (life 40 f, α .5). Flicker death (last 70 f): flame height × `(.55+.45·[fbm(T*f)>thr])` with `f=.3+1.2(1-lit/70)` (deterministic stutter, accelerating) + tick `snd(1200,.02,'square',.015)` every 12 f; light follows. Re-light: flame `outBack` 12 f, light 60→180 in 10 f, ring.
**A12 Spectral slab.** Ghost: dashed bone-blue outline, fill α .06-.1, motes falling *through* it (non-solid cue), breathing rune. Materialise (14 f): 8 horizontal bands, 1 f stagger top→bottom, band goes outline→solid stone, a white-cyan scanline sweeps down once (`outQuart`), 2×2 dither mask flicker, settle pulse. Dematerialise: reverse with glitch stutter in the 70 f flicker; final collapse into 12 motes (`Pool`) drifting up. Lit slab spills `th.a` light under itself (24 px additive gradient).
**A13 Storm strike** (`strike:tell|hit|pulse`).
 · TELL 54 f: f0 first bell toll `snd(196,1.6,'sine',.05)+snd(392,1.2,'triangle',.025)+snd(588,.8,'sine',.012)`, bell pendulum amplitude 0.5 rad (`A*=.992`/tick); sky dips α .08; rumble `snd(48,.8,'sine',.05)`; rod corona r 12→34 (`inQuad`) + copper-tip sparks; ground ring (r 38, dashed, rotating .06 rad/f, α .3→.8); the **leader** (`leader` depth 6) grows from the clouds, revealed fraction `inQuad(u)` with a bright head (r 6); first 60 % of vertices stay fixed (no chaos), the tail reseeds every 3 f.
 · HIT 8 f: f0 full bolt (`lgBolt` `{j:.09,d:7,nb:8}`): white core w5 + cyan glow w16 + violet outer w30 α .25; `gvJuice({fl:16,shake:18,zm:.05,hs:3})`; `noise(.9,.18,1800)+snd(42,1.1,'sawtooth',.1,-18)`; second bell toll f4; f2-8 thickness 5→1 with branch flicker.
 · AFTER 30 f: bolt afterimage violet α .35→0 (`outQuart`); rod tip orange-white cooling (`tl`); scorch decal (≤ 6 kept); 12 falling sparks; shockwave ring `m 140` + elliptical ground ripple through pools; rain brightens +.2 for 6 f.
**A14 Rain.** Two cached 256² streak tiles (speed 14 / 9 px/f, shear 12°, α .18 / .1), ≤ 8 splash crowns per frame (hash of `(platform,slot,T>>3)`, 3 sprites, life 6 f), expanding ellipses in pools, wet platform tops (rim line + 10 px faint reflection).
**A15 Backdrop life.**
 · Stage 11: fractal trees baked in 2 canvases (trunk + coarse branches / fine branches ≥ depth 4); the fine layer drawn with `rotate(.012·sin(tick*.02+id)+gust)` about the trunk top (`gust=fbm(tick*.004)`). 3 fog bands = tiled noise scrolled .1/.25/.4 px/f. 12 wisps on Lissajous orbits that brighten at 100 px from the player and drift away (smooth, deterministic). **A16 Stage title card** (first 150 f of the stage, drawn after `gvDark`, input never blocked). Title text revealed by a left→right clip with a ragged noisy edge (`fbm` per column) over 40 f + additive `th.a` glow; beneath it a root/Sierpiński flourish grows from the centre (`rootSegs` depth 4, one depth per 12 f); "STAGE n" fades in 20 f later; hold 50 f; fade 24 f `outQuart`; audio: low bell + rising filtered `noise` swell.
**A17 Clear variants (11-13).** All slabs discharge in a cascade (3 f stagger by x) with chain bolts linking neighbours (A05); 24 soul motes rise from tombstones; darkness α → 0 over 60 f (`inOutSine`) — the world is revealed; bell toll; optional banner line `The dark recedes...`.
**A18 Hint pops.** `pop()` text + focus ring (`ring m 70`, `th.a`) around the object, which pulses scale 1→1.08→1 (`pulse`, 24 f); ≤ 1 hint per 240 f; once per run per key.
**A19 Camera & time.** Trauma values: slab discharge 6, zap 12 (built-in), strike 18; cap 24. `zm ≤ .06`, `hs ≤ 4`. At most one "big" event per 90 f — the storm director delays a strike that would overlap a discharge/zap. Nothing during pause/cutscene.
**A20 Darkness breathing.** Player light `r=210·(1+.02·sin(T*.07))`; at `hp==1` heartbeat every 50 f: contract 8 % at f0-4 (lub), 4 % at f12-16 (dub), vignette α +.06 on lub; edge shimmer `r*=1+(fbm(T*.5,id)-.5)·.03`; lights entering from off-screen ease in over 10 f; strike reveal `α*(1-fl/16)`.

## Definition of Done for every animation
(1) has anticipation + settle where physical; (2) readable at α .78 darkness; (3) deterministic (same `T` ⇒ same pixels); (4) pooled/cached, zero per-frame allocation, cost within GV_ENGINE_SPEC §5; (5) audio cue on the event frame; (6) works at `FXON==0` (reduced) and in the stage-select preview (static pose); (7) screenshot captured and inspected; (8) listed in `HANDOFF.md` with its measured ms.
